operational policy files and tracked evaluation documents under tests/evaluations/.
Each has one primary category. Historical evidence remains valid for its stated
scope; historical does not mean failed or superseded. Only replaced rules/status
are labeled superseded and linked to their replacement. Partially implemented
proposals are indexed as historical design records with their current status.

Add every future document and dated evaluation in the same change, per the
[development standard](DEVELOPMENT_CONTINUITY_STANDARD.md#documentation-navigation).
Prefer updating an existing active document over creating another dated summary.
Private evidence must stay outside the public index. State local verification,
deployment, manual acceptance and scheduled acceptance separately.
